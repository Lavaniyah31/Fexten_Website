Our Solutions

A comprehensive suite of cloud-based ERP solutions to meet the diverse needs of micro, small, and medium scale businesses. Our products are designed to streamline your operations, improve decision-making, and fuel growth.

ERP Cloud MINI

A basic ERP for startups and micro businesses to handle daily operations quickly and efficiently.

Designed specifically for those taking their first steps into digital management, FEXTEN MINI provides the essential core features needed to keep your business running smoothly without unnecessary complexity.

ERP Cloud LITE

A simple and reliable ERP solution designed for small businesses seeking efficiency and ease of use.

FEXTEN LITE offers a balanced set of tools to manage inventory, sales, and customers, helping small businesses grow with confidence and clarity.

ERP Cloud PREMIUM

A powerful solution for medium businesses needing advanced features, high scalability, and full control across complex operations.

Our flagship cloud offering provides end-to-end integration, advanced analytics, and multi-departmental workflows to support rapidly scaling enterprises.

ERP Local SATC

A reliable on-premises ERP solution designed for businesses seeking complete local control and dependable performance.

For organizations that prefer hosting data on their own hardware, SATC delivers the power of FEXTEN with the security and governance of a local installation.

ERP Custom Edition

A fully personalized ERP solution crafted to fit the unique needs of each business, ensuring seamless integration and adaptability.

We work closely with you to understand your unique business processes and build a system that fits like a glove.
